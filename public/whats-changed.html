What's changed

This page lists the changes to the NYC Executive Orders explorer that a reader would notice: new tabs and tools, fixes, and corrections when we published something wrong. Newest first. The orders themselves come from BetaNYC's executive orders corpus, which is still being checked and filled in, so routine updates to the orders are not listed here.

  • History begins: 2026-08-18, the day the explorer went live. Corrections made before this date may be missing.
  • Data refresh: whenever the site is rebuilt, from the latest copy of the corpus. There is no fixed schedule.

To report a bug in this site or ask for a new feature, open an issue at https://github.com/BetaNYC/nyc-eo-explorer/issues.

September 9, 2026

New Agencies tab shows which orders name each City agency

September 8, 2026

Tags: Site, Data

A new Agencies tab lists every City agency or office the orders name, with a page for each one: the orders that name it, year by year, and the agencies most often named alongside it. Names that match BetaNYC's list of current agencies are marked Confirmed. Other names, including bodies that no longer exist, are marked Proposed, because nobody has reviewed them yet.

Who it affects: Anyone looking up an agency's history in the orders. The list shows every name it found, including names seen in only one order.

The explorer went live

August 18, 2026

Tags: Site, Data

A free website to search and browse New York City mayoral executive orders, with no account needed. Search the full text of every order, filter by mayor and year, and read each order beside a scan of the original. A Pictures tab shows the seals and signatures clipped from the oldest scans.
